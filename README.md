## Hi, I'm Lili Fanni Tóth

I build software that makes clinical data trustworthy: pipelines, quality
control, and ML models for magnetic resonance spectroscopy of brain tumors.
PhD candidate at Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (thesis deposited;
defense December 3, 2026).

**What I've built** (all with DOIs and documentation; in use by my group
and a neighbouring research group):
- [MRSprep](https://github.com/lilively/MRSprep) — ETL and QC pipeline for
  clinical MRS data: DICOM (pydicom), SPAR/SDAT, jMRUI; ~2000 cases (Python)
- [MRSplotter](https://github.com/lilively/MRSplotter) — GUI app for
  publication-ready MRS visualization (Python/PyQt6, Windows binary)
- [MRScoreg](https://github.com/lilively/MRScoreg) — DICOM→NIfTI conversion
  and MRS voxel-to-anatomy coregistration (MATLAB, compiled release)
- [MRSdecomposer](https://doi.org/10.34810/DATA3421) — metabolic pattern
  analysis for MRS (MATLAB, compiled release); poster at the ISMRM MRS
  Workshop 2026; basis of a collaborator's ISMRM 2026 study
